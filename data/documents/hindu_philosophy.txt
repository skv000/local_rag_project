Hindu philosophy contains several major philosophical traditions.

Advaita Vedanta teaches non-duality and emphasizes the relationship between Atman and Brahman.

Samkhya philosophy describes reality through the distinction between Purusha, consciousness, and Prakriti, material nature.

Yoga philosophy focuses on disciplined practice, meditation, concentration, and liberation.

Bhakti traditions emphasize devotion and a personal relationship with the divine.

The Bhagavad Gita discusses concepts including dharma, karma, yoga, devotion, knowledge, and liberation.
